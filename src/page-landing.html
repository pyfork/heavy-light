What is Halfway?

A city pass built for six to seventy-two hours.

Most city passes assume a week. A layover is an afternoon, a night, maybe two. Halfway starts the clock when you clear the airport and stops it when you check in again, so you only pay for the hours you are actually in town.

One pass covers the train in, the metro around, the big sights and a left-luggage locker by the station. The app lays out what fits before your next flight and books the entry slots for you.

How it works

How it works

Three steps, no queues.

  1. 01

    Land and drop your bag

    Show the pass at the airport rail gate. Your locker code arrives before the train does.

  2. 02

    Tell the app your next flight

    It plans a loop that gets you back to the gate with an hour to spare, and books the timed entries.

  3. 03

    Go and look closer

    Scan in at each door. If a flight moves, the plan moves with it; unused hours roll over for a year.

F.A.Q.

When does the clock start?

At the first scan: the airport rail gate, a metro barrier or a museum door, whichever comes first.

Buying early costs nothing; the pass sleeps until you use it.

What if my flight is delayed?

Tell the app your new departure time. It reshuffles the plan, and any hours you could not use roll over for twelve months.

Do I need a visa to leave the airport?

That depends on your passport and the city, not on the pass. Check the official immigration rules before you fly; the app links to each one.

Can I share a pass?

Each traveller needs their own, but one phone can hold a whole family's passes.
